# Creator Hub Template Submissions

## Template 1: AI Weekly Client Report Generator (Marketing Agencies)
**Category:** Marketing
**Tags:** marketing, reporting, ai, email, agency, automation
**Description:**
Automatically generate and email weekly performance reports to your clients. Connects to your data source (Google Sheets, Google Ads, Meta API), uses Gemini AI to write an insightful narrative summary with trends and recommendations, then sends a professional email to each client on a schedule. Perfect for marketing agencies managing 5-50 clients who spend hours on manual reporting.

**Setup:** Replace the two "edit me" Code nodes with your data sources. Add your Gemini API key and Gmail OAuth. Set the schedule. Done.

## Template 2: AI Lead Scorer & Slack Alerter (Webhook)
**Category:** Sales
**Tags:** sales, lead-scoring, ai, webhook, slack, crm
**Description:**
Point your website form at this webhook and every new lead gets AI-scored 0-100 in real time. HOT leads (70+) trigger an instant Slack alert to your sales team with full context. Returns the score as JSON so your form can show a thank-you message. Built for speed-to-lead: the whole flow runs in under 5 seconds.

**Setup:** Add your Gemini API key and Slack OAuth. Replace the Slack channel ID. Point your form's POST URL to the webhook endpoint. Done.

## Template 3: AI Invoice Data Extractor → Google Sheets
**Category:** Finance
**Tags:** finance, invoice, extraction, ai, ocr, accounts-payable
**Description:**
Paste any invoice text (or connect a Gmail trigger for PDF attachments) and Gemini AI extracts every field: vendor, invoice number, dates, line items, subtotal, tax, total, payment terms. Structured JSON output logged directly to a Google Sheets ledger. Saves 3-6 hours/week of manual data entry. Catches the fields humans miss.

**Setup:** Add your Gemini API key and Google Sheets OAuth. Replace the sheet ID. For production, swap the manual trigger for a Gmail Trigger watching invoice emails.
